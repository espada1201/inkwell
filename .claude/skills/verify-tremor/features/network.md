# Network

The Network screen answers "was that a quake, near me?". A card lists what was reported near the reader's location, nearest first. Below it, the screen counts the phones listening on the relays this browser follows. It turns their picks into a provisional detection within seconds and their vetted triggers into a confirmed, located quake, and checks each against the USGS feed, which it also lists. Anyone can report shaking they felt, and an opt-in chime sounds for shaking near the reader that phones confirmed or USGS lists, while the tab is open. The screen ignores forged or malformed events, and the reader can change the relays in use.

## Sub-features

- `net-near` asks once for a location (a town's name, typed coordinates, or the browser's) and keeps it. Towns come from a list bundled with the page (15,000+ people, 5,000+ in the US), loaded only when a name is typed; case, accents, apostrophes and letters like ł or ß do not matter, and a state, country code or `UK` after a comma narrows it; several of one name are offered as choices, the six biggest first (a count says when there are more), and a named town is shown and measured from by name. It lists Tremor detections and felt shakings within 100 km, and USGS quakes within 300 km that would have been felt there at least weakly, nearest first. Each row has the distance and a rough local shaking word; quakes below M2.5 read weak at most. While this phone shares as a sensor, the card links to `#sensor` instead of offering `change`, since moving it would misplace the phone's picks. After light or stronger shaking it adds Drop, Cover and Hold On and a link to `#sensor`. With nothing near, it says that nothing strong enough to feel was reported, and how soon USGS usually posts.
- `net-phones` counts phones from their heartbeats; a phone that stopped or went quiet stops counting. Signatures are checked in a few web workers, and the quakes are worked out in another (the page keeps only what changed in step with it, and does the work itself where workers are missing), so a network of thousands of phones does not freeze the page; the screen redraws a few times a second, and the model waits at least three times as long as its last round took before the next, so a big network answers a little later, never not at all.
- `net-provisional` shows a provisional detection as an alert, from raw picks that fit one wavefront, with how far off its place could be. While the page stays open it keeps the detection listed for two hours with how it ended (a reload starts the list afresh): confirmed, or not confirmed with the phones' reasons.
- `net-confirmed` lists a confirmed quake from vetted triggers, with its place and size, marked `Matches USGS` when the feed has it. Selecting it moves focus to the comparison, scrolled into view, with how far it is from the location set: how far off the place (`± N km`, and a dashed ring on the map) and the magnitude (a range) could be, nine times in ten as measured on simulated quakes, and that big quakes read low.
- `net-felt` takes an "I felt it" report from the header or the card. The reader says when (just now, or 5, 15 or 30 min ago) and picks one of five levels with one tap, at most once per key per ten minutes of shaking; the report carries the time it shook, and a press beside the dialog closes it (not one on its padding, nor a drag that ends outside). A second report within ten minutes of the first's shaking is refused, with the time of the first. The reader then gets a `Tell USGS too` link to the matching USGS event or to Did You Feel It. A shaking shows once three people in three places agree; one key reporting from many places shows nothing.
- `net-chime` works only after the reader ticks the box. It then sounds, vibrates, notifies and shows a dismissable alert once for each light or stronger shaking near the reader that phones confirmed or USGS lists. It does none of this for withdrawn picks, provisional detections, what people say they felt, weak shaking, shaking listed before the tick or shaking older than 30 minutes.
- `net-usgs` lists the USGS feed for the past day or hour, nearest first or worldwide, with placeholders while it loads, the 12 nearest or newest and a line saying how many there are in all, a line saying it retries when the feed cannot be reached, and the note that USGS may miss quakes below about M4.5 outside the US.
- `net-hostile` drops forged and malformed events without breaking the page.
- `net-relays` counts open relays, says so when none can be reached, and edits the list. A relay that is down is tried again after 1 s, 2 s, 4 s and so on, but at least every 5 minutes, and at once when the browser comes back online; the browser's own console logs one error per refused try. It saves only `wss://` addresses (or `ws://` to localhost), one per line or comma-separated as a link writes them; the saved list survives a reload (carried in the address where the browser refuses to store it), and a `?relays=` link with no usable address falls back to the usual relays.
- `net-offline-map` falls back to a bundled coastline when map tiles cannot load.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The `Network` tab, the `TREMOR` home link, or `#network`.
- The header's `I felt it` button (any screen) and the relay count (`relay-status`).
- `?relays=wss://a,wss://b` before the hash.

## Driving it with drive.mjs

Preconditions: the baseline. Start the journey with `const { scenarioEvents, send } = await app('/tests/e2e/publish.ts')` and `const napa = scenarioEvents('napa-2014')`, which ends the Napa quake 80 s ago. For `net-confirmed`, add USGS's own record before opening: `usgs([{ id: 'nc-replayed', mag: 6, place: '6 km NW of American Canyon, CA', time: napa.sim.origin, lat: 38.215, lon: -122.312, depth: 11 }])`. Felt reports and extra phones come from `app('/src/net/protocol.ts')` (`signFelt`, `signHeartbeat`, `signPick`, `signTrigger`, `signRetraction`) with fresh keys from `nostr-tools/pure`'s `generateSecretKey`.

- **Near you, typed (`net-near`).** `open('#network')`. In `near`, fill `Or type a town, or latitude, longitude` with `38.7`: the `Set` button turns dark (it stands out while the box holds text not yet used) and goes back when the box is emptied. Press `Set`: the status says `Type a town, or a latitude and a longitude…` and the box is `aria-invalid`. Fill `San Rafael` and press Enter: choices `San Rafael, AR` (focused) and `San Rafael, CA, US`; pick the second: `Near San Rafael, CA, US (37.97°N 122.53°W)` and rows read `km from San Rafael, CA, US`, `tremor.place` holds the name. `Lodz` finds `Łódź, PL`, `Washington, D.C.` one town, `London, UK` only `London, GB`, and `Springfield` six choices under `N towns match; here are the 6 biggest`. `Qqqqzz` says `No town called`. Fill `38.7, -122.7` and press `Set`: `Near 38.70°N 122.70°W`, `change` takes focus, `tremor.location` is `{ lat: 38.7, lon: -122.7 }`, and the `near-item`s run nearest first, each with `km from you`.
- **Near you, browser location (`net-near`).** Call `page.context().grantPermissions(['geolocation'])` and `setGeolocation(...)` before pressing `change`, then press `Use my approximate location`: the card follows. Reload: the card keeps it. With `tremor.join` set to `1` and a reload, `change` is gone and `change it under Be a sensor` links to `#sensor`.
- **Phones (`net-phones`).** `relay-status` reads `1/1 relays`, `no-events` shows. `send(relay.url, napa.heartbeats)`: `stat-nodes` reads their count.
- **Provisional (`net-provisional`).** `send(relay.url, napa.picks)`: `provisional` shows and contains `± N km`, and `getByRole('alert')` contains `Provisional earthquake detection`. For the withdrawn path, publish four nearby phones' heartbeats and picks, then a `footsteps` retraction from each: `outcome` reads `Not confirmed: 4 of 4 phones withdrew (footsteps…`.
- **Confirmed (`net-confirmed`).** `send(relay.url, napa.finals)`: `stat-events` reads `1`, the provisional card goes, `outcome` starts `Confirmed: M`, `event-list` contains `Matches USGS`. Press the event's button (rows end `compare ›`): `compare` takes focus in view, `detail-km` reads `N km from you` (or from the town), and it shows `est-loc` within about 15 km of Napa, `est-km` reading `± N km` with the USGS epicentre within N km, `est-mag` reading a range that holds 6.0, a note starting `In tests on simulated quakes, nine times in ten`, and a `verdict` starting `Off by`. The map shows a dashed ring labelled `± N km`.
- **Felt (`net-felt`).** With a location set, press the banner's `I felt it`: dialog `I felt it` with five buttons in `How strong was the shaking where you are?`. Press `15 min ago` in `When was it?`, then `light…`: the status says `You reported light shaking at` with a time zone, the event's `t` is 15 min back, and `relay.events()` holds one kind-4580 event with `lvl: 2` and the location to 0.01°. Close, press the card's `I felt it`: `You can report again from` and no new event. A drag from inside the dialog to the page, and a press on its padding, leave it open; a press on the page closes it. After a report at `30 min ago` and a reload, another at `30 min ago` says `You already reported light shaking at` and sends nothing; `Just now` then sends. The same holds with site storage blocked (`addInitScript` making `localStorage` throw). Publish three reports from fresh keys 4 km apart: one `felt` row, `3 people in 3 places felt shaking`. Twelve from one key in twelve places: no row.
- **Chime (`net-chime`).** Before `open`, replace `AudioContext`, `Notification` and `navigator.vibrate` with counters (`addInitScript`), as `tests/e2e/network.spec.ts` does. Set a location, tick `Chime when shaking is confirmed near me`. Picks that are then withdrawn: no tone. Four phones within 100 km confirm a quake: one tone, one buzz, one notification, and an alert `Shaking reported near you. Not an official warning.` with `Dismiss`. People then report the same shaking, or very strong shaking elsewhere nearby: still one. A USGS quake that shook here 40 minutes ago: still one; one 12 minutes ago (`past hour`/`past day` refetches the feed): two.
- **USGS (`net-usgs`).** `usgs-list` has one `li` per row of the feed up to 12, then a line `The 12 nearest of N are shown.`, nearest first once a location is set; `worldwide` reorders it. Press `past hour`: the button is pressed and the list follows the shorter feed (`usgs(rows)` answers both feeds alike, so route `all_hour` yourself to see the difference). With `page.route` answering the feed with `garbage`, the screen says `The USGS feed could not be reached`.
- **Hostile (`net-hostile`).** The helper's relay checks signatures and drops forgeries before the page sees them, so serve this through a relay of your own that forwards anything (as `tests/e2e/network.spec.ts` does with `verify: false`). Send a heartbeat whose content was edited after signing and one with an invalid signature: `stat-nodes` does not count them and no console error appears.
- **Relays (`net-relays`).** Open `details.relays`. Type `relay.damus.io`, `wss://nos.lol` and `ws://relay.example.com` in `Relay URLs, one per line` and press `Save and reconnect`: a status names `relay.damus.io` and `ws://relay.example.com`, the box is `aria-invalid`, nothing is saved. With two `wss://` lines the page reloads with both in `tremor.relays`; with `Storage.prototype.setItem` throwing (`addInitScript`), it reloads with both in `?relays=` and `tremor.relays` stays empty. For no relay at all, `page.goto` the served URL with `?relays=wss://relay.one.example#network` (`open` would put the private relay first): `0/1 relays` and a status `Not connected to any relay…`, never "all quiet".
- **Offline map (`net-offline-map`).** Any isolated run: the `Offline basemap` note shows and the map still draws.

## Gotchas

- Events from a phone whose heartbeat has not arrived wait until it does; send heartbeats first or in the same batch.
- The screen recomputes 200 ms after a batch arrives; wait on the counts, not on time.
- `scenarioEvents` dates the quake from the moment it is called; its Napa picks are already 30 to 60 s old, and the early detector uses picks up to 60 s old, so send them within about 30 s of calling it or no provisional detection shows. The near-you card lists Tremor detections for 10 minutes only.
- The chime counts only shaking that arrives after the box is ticked, and the tick is not saved across reloads: a page can start sound only from a tap.
- With no relay connected and the USGS feed unreachable, the near-you card says it cannot tell whether anything shook, never that nothing did.
- A `?relays=` link overrides the saved list until the reader saves the list again from the editor.
- Mocks used here, beyond the baseline: the browser's geolocation (`net-near`), and the speaker, vibration and notifications (`net-chime`). Name them in the proof.
